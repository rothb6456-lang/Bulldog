create-team.html

desktop-first

not dependent on backend functionality
not intended to simulate all production behaviors
Current Prototype Quality Goals
The prototype should demonstrate:

strong product hierarchy
visible Bulldog brand alignment
desktop confidence
readable tables/forms/cards
credible navigation
a clear app shell/sidebar/header system
serious and restrained software feel
internal consistency across pages
Most importantly:

team-detail.html must be the strongest page
It should feel like:

the operational heart of the product
the place where overview, roster, roles, and actions come together
the page that makes the product concept “click”
Current Brand Direction
The prototype should feel like a software extension of bulldogstats.com.

It should use:

dark/navy primary framing
Bulldog green as accent/action color
clean neutrals
strong contrast
confident headings
restrained logo usage
serious, premium-but-practical product styling
It should avoid:

generic starter dashboard feel
random placeholder colors
fake mascot art
clip-art
collage-style screenshots in the UI
overly playful visual treatment
Screenshots from bulldogstats.com are reference material, not decorative page content.

Current Asset Policy
If official Bulldog logo/wordmark assets exist:

use them carefully
preserve proportions
avoid distortion or improvised recoloring
If official assets do not exist:

use restrained text-based brand treatment instead
Do not:

invent logos
use AI-generated mascot replacements
use generic sports clip-art
Assets belong in:

05_Prototype-Assets/screenshots/

Current Acceptance Standard
The prototype is considered successful when it is strong enough for confident product review, not necessarily production completeness.

The key acceptance questions are:

Does this feel like Bulldog?
Does this feel like a real product rather than a concept sketch?
Does the desktop experience feel credible?
Does the team-detail page make the product make sense?
Would this help guide real Laravel implementation later?
If the answer is yes to those questions, the prototype is likely in good shape.

What Has Been Decided

Documentation decisions
