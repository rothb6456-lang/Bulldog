\# Prototype Review Checklist  
<br/>Version: v1.0  
Last Updated: 2026-08-18  
Status: Active  
Document Type: Review Checklist  
Priority: High  
<br/>\## Purpose  
<br/>This checklist is used to review the static Bulldog Statbook prototype after generation or refinement.  
<br/>\---  
<br/>\## Brand Fit  
<br/>\- \[ \] Feels clearly related to bulldogstats.com  
\- \[ \] Does not feel like a generic admin template  
\- \[ \] Uses Bulldog green and dark/navy framing appropriately  
\- \[ \] Uses logo/wordmark correctly or uses text fallback cleanly  
\- \[ \] Does not use fake mascot/clip-art substitutions  
<br/>\---  
<br/>\## Desktop Experience  
<br/>\- \[ \] Layout feels desktop-first  
\- \[ \] Page spacing feels intentional  
\- \[ \] Information hierarchy is easy to scan  
\- \[ \] Sidebar/header feel product-grade  
\- \[ \] Tables are usable and not cramped  
\- \[ \] Team detail page feels like the main work surface  
<br/>\---  
<br/>\## Team Detail Page  
<br/>\- \[ \] Team header is strong  
\- \[ \] Overview / Roster / Roles are clearly distinct  
\- \[ \] Roster table is easy to understand  
\- \[ \] Role table is easy to understand  
\- \[ \] Tabs or sections feel polished  
\- \[ \] This is the strongest page in the prototype  
<br/>\---  
<br/>\## Page Completeness  
<br/>\- \[ \] index.html feels like a real product entry page  
\- \[ \] dashboard.html feels structured and useful  
\- \[ \] teams.html feels coherent and navigable  
\- \[ \] create-team.html feels product-grade  
\- \[ \] player-identity.html feels like an identity record  
\- \[ \] guardian-relationships.html feels clear and credible  
<br/>\---  
<br/>\## UI Components  
<br/>\- \[ \] Buttons have clear hierarchy  
\- \[ \] Forms feel intentionally styled  
\- \[ \] Badges are distinct and readable  
\- \[ \] Active / pending / claimed / unclaimed states read clearly  
\- \[ \] Team Admin / Coach / Viewer role badges feel intentional  
<br/>\---  
<br/>\## Tone  
<br/>\- \[ \] Copy feels product-oriented  
\- \[ \] Copy is not overly fluffy or generic  
\- \[ \] The product feels serious, structured, and practical  
\- \[ \] The interface does not feel childish, noisy, or gimmicky  
<br/>\---  
<br/>\## Technical / Delivery  
<br/>\- \[ \] Static HTML/CSS/JS only  
\- \[ \] No backend dependencies introduced  
\- \[ \] Easy to deploy to Cloudflare Pages  
\- \[ \] Files are clean and easy to edit
