## Purpose

This document defines the sample content and placeholder data that should be used consistently across the Bulldog Statbook static prototype.

The goal is to make the prototype feel like one coherent product instead of a collection of disconnected mock pages.

This file helps Codex keep:
- team names consistent
- people/roles consistent
- player identities consistent
- statuses consistent
- tone aligned with the Bulldog brand
- sample copy credible and product-oriented

---

## Content Principles

Sample content should feel:

- realistic
- structured
- coach/admin relevant
- brand-aligned
- clear and consistent

Sample content should not feel:

- random
- overly playful
- fake in an obvious way
- overloaded with lorem ipsum
- cluttered with unnecessary names or metrics

---

## Brand Tone Reminder

The content tone should align with Bulldog and bulldogstats.com.

Use language that feels:

- confident
- concise
- sports-operations focused
- practical
- coach-built
- team-first

Avoid language that feels:

- overly generic SaaS
- fluffy marketing filler
- childish
- “social app” oriented
- overly technical for no reason

## Content Consistency Rules

When generating or refining prototype pages:

1. Use **Bulldogs 12U** as the primary team
2. Keep **Jordan Hayes** as the primary signed-in user
3. Reuse the same player codes across pages where appropriate
4. Keep role/status labels consistent
5. Prefer fewer, more believable records over many random fake records
6. Keep copy concise and product-oriented

---

## Success Condition

This sample content guide is working if:
- the same team appears consistently across pages
- the same users/roles appear consistently across pages
- player records feel connected across screens
- the prototype feels coherent and believable
- the copy sounds like Bulldog, not a generic UI kit
